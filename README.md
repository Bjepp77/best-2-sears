# The Best Two Sears

A phone-first cooking guide for missionaries in a new area: local stores and staples, simple healthy recipes for the kitchen you actually have, a week of meals built on local food, and basic food and water safety. Covers parts of Andean South America, the Philippines, Anglophone West Africa, and Mexico and Central America so far.

Your area and kitchen setup stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no tracking, and nothing is sent anywhere. Works offline once loaded.

An independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints or any mission.

Not medical advice. For food allergies, diabetes, special diets or illness, talk to your mission nurse or mission leaders. Prices are rough estimates with the date they were checked, never exact. Every fact links to the source it came from.

This repository holds the built static site only.
